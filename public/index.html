One job. Done properly.

TNT Hopper Protect changes the interaction that matters while leaving the rest of Minecraft's explosion behavior alone.

🛒

Protect hopper minecarts

Explosion damage that would destroy a hopper minecart is blocked by the plugin.

💥

Keep TNT behavior

TNT still detonates and can break ordinary blocks. The explosion itself is not cancelled.

⚡

Lightweight

No accounts, client mods, or extra dependencies. Install the JAR and configure it for your server.
